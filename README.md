# ICT-PracticeWhat ICT means to me
ICT (Information and Communication Technologies) is the bridge between an idea and the world. It is how I learn new skills, share my work, and connect with people who can guide me.
For a mechatronics student, ICT is also the foundation of everything I build. Number systems, data, and code are the language that machines use, and understanding them helps me design smarter systems that combine mechanics, electronics, and software.
Tools like GitHub, LinkedIn, and Gmail help me present my work professionally, find mentors, and grow in a global community. To me, ICT is not just a subject. It is a way to keep learning, stay connected, and turn my passion for engineering into real projects.
